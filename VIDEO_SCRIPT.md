# Amazon Hive — Demo Video Script (target: 2:45, hard limit 3:00)

Rules check: under 3 minutes, English, public on YouTube/Vimeo, shows the project
functioning, no unlicensed music (use YouTube's free audio library or none).

## 0:00–0:20 — The problem (Sabrina on camera or voiceover over the demo)
**Say:** "Your home hears everything and remembers nothing. You tell your
phone the cleaner's coming at three, and by three you've forgotten — and the
doorbell still scares you out of your chair. Amazon Hive fixes that."

## 0:20–0:40 — Meet Hive
**Show:** demo header + the four device cards.
**Say:** "Hive is an ambient-intelligence hub. Bee captures what you say out
in the world. Ring is the perimeter. Alexa+ routes and announces. Fire TV is
the screen. And Hive is the brain that connects them."

## 0:40–1:20 — The visitor story (the core demo)
**Show:** Run step 1 → step 2, let 3–5 play out on screen.
**Say:** "Watch. I tell Bee, 'I'm expecting the cleaner at three.' Bee logs
it. When Ring sees a person at the door, Hive already knows who it should
be — so Alexa+ doesn't just say 'motion at the front door,' she says, 'Your
cleaner is here.' Fire TV shows the door, and Bee writes it into my day.
One story, four devices, zero phone-checking."

## 1:20–1:50 — Brain Dump Dashboard
**Show:** Tap "Show my brain dump" — the Fire TV dashboard.
**Say:** "At the end of the day I say, 'Alexa, show my brain dump.' Fire TV
pulls up everything Bee caught — ideas, errands, who's expected — next to
the day's Ring events. My whole mental load, on the big screen, sorted."

## 1:50–2:20 — Focus Mode
**Show:** Turn Focus Mode on, run the door event again. Quiet PiP appears.
**Say:** "When I'm studying, I tap Bee for Focus Mode. The house goes quiet.
If someone comes to the door, there's no announcement blasting through the
house — Fire TV shows a small, silent picture-in-picture, and Bee still logs
the visit. Quiet, but never blind."

## 2:20–2:45 — What's real, what's next
**Show:** the MCP inspector panel; then the Hive base concept panel.
**Say:** "What's real today: a working MCP server that Alexa+ tools actually
call, and a Ring webhook receiver with real signature verification. The
device feeds are simulated and labeled, and the next step is the Hive Base —
a countertop dock. Drop Bee in when you walk in, and docking hands your day
to the house."

## 2:45–3:00 — Close
**Say:** "Hive is for anyone whose brain is full and whose door matters —
caregivers, parents, people who can't just get up and check. It's the home
that thinks ahead. Thank you."
